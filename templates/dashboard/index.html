Transparent Scoring Rules & Methodology

The Final Startup Verdict is generated through a rigorous weighted multi-factor model synthesized from all 8 validation agents. No subjective guessing is used.

Scoring Dimension Weight Evaluation Benchmark Threshold Rules
Market Opportunity 30% TAM > $1B, CAGR > 12%, identified high-intent buyer pain points. >80 = Massive headroom; <50 = Saturated or niche
Competitive Moat 25% Gaps in incumbent pricing, usability, self-serve speed, and automation. >75 = Defensible differentiation; <50 = Red ocean copycat
Execution Feasibility 25% Clarity of 6-week MVP roadmap, low technical hurdles, clear core loop. >80 = Rapid 6-wk launch; <50 = Complex R&D bottleneck
Risk Resistance 20% Absence of existential regulatory, high-CAC, or operational risks. >70 = Manageable headwinds; <40 = High vulnerability
Recommendation Decision Thresholds:
PROCEED (Score ≥ 75) Strong validation across market, defensibility, and execution. Immediate recommendation to build MVP.
IMPROVE (Score 55 - 74) Viable core concept, but requires strategic iteration on customer acquisition, pricing, or defensibility before building.
RECONSIDER (Score < 55) Elevated structural headwinds or excessive competitor moats. Recommend pivoting the core value proposition.
